# Candidates & internal tournament — news-image-20261126 (v2 resubmit)

## What v1 got wrong — the reason for v2

v1's hero showed the 100th Macy's Thanksgiving Day Parade filling Manhattan
with giant balloons on Thursday 26 November 2026. **That did not happen.**
Late on Wednesday 25 November the parade was **postponed to Saturday
28 November** because of a nor'easter — the first postponement for weather
in the parade's 100-year history (Reuters, CNN, AP, USA Today, WSJ; the
postponement story itself was published 2026-11-26T01:46:00Z). Balloons
inflated on Wednesday afternoon were deflated the same evening; NBC aired
a rerun of the 2025 parade on Thanksgiving Day instead. High-wind warnings
ran all day Thursday, gusts up to 60 mph. So v1's hero picture is false
for the day it claims to sum up (judge criterion 4: nothing the judge
knows to be false), and its most prominent story is an event that never
took place that day. v2 corrects the record and makes the postponement
itself the hero — the most date-specific story of 26 November 2026.
