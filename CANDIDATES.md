## Pairwise tournament (judge brief, weight order: 1 date+day's news, 2 selection/breadth, 3 reads, 4 faithful, 5 craft)

Round 1:
- 1 vs 2: both name the day, both broad. 1 wins on 2: parade hero is the day's most
  recognisable date-anchored event (Thanksgiving IS 26 Nov; the 100th parade is unique
  to this day), while 2's hero (astronaut) is reported widely but less date-distinctive.
  1 advances.
- 3 vs 4: 3's map is clever but pins are small and hard to read at 300px (criterion 5);
  4's war hero is strong but leads with one conflict, narrowing breadth (criterion 2).
  4 advances on 3 (reads) — map loses on legibility.
- 5 vs 6: 5 leads with a refinery fire — dramatic but one-beat; 6's timeline reads well

- 7 vs 8: 7 is one picture + teasers — weak on criterion 2 (only one visual story);

- 9 vs 10: 9 repeats 2's astronaut hero (loses to 1's logic); 10's split gives the parade
  half the page and four stacked stories — good breadth, clear reading. 10 advances.
- 11 vs 12: 11's war-heavy top narrows the day to two conflicts (criterion 2);
  12 is clean but only 3 stories — too thin for criterion 2. 11 advances (breadth 5 > 3).

Round 2:
